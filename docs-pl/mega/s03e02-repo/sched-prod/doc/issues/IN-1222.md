## Intent
An account can create slots of 15 minutes. Today the smallest slot is 30 minutes. A client (IN-1222) and a support ticket (IN-6401) ask for the same thing.

## Acceptance
- Given an account editing its availability, when it picks a 15-minute length, then 15-minute slots appear on the booking page.
- Given existing 30-minute slots, when the change ships, then they and their bookings stay exactly as they are, not split or moved.
- Given a 15-minute slot, when it is booked, then reminders and the export carry its real start and end in UTC (PRD-2).
- A test covers a 15-minute slot from creation to booking.

## Boundaries
- Must not change existing slots or bookings.
- Must not change prices or plans.

Allowed by AUT-1 (small features inside the specs that add to what is there and take nothing away).
