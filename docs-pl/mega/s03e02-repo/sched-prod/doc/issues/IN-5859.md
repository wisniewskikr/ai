## Intent
Hovering over a booking in the team's week view shows the name of the person who booked it, next to its time.

## Acceptance
- Given a booking in week view, when a team member hovers over it, then the tooltip shows its time and the name of the person who booked.
- Given a booking without a name, when hovered, then the tooltip shows its time only and not an empty field.
- Given the public booking page, when a slot is hovered, then the names of other people are not shown.

## Boundaries
- Must not show names on the public booking page.
- Must not change the booking data.

Allowed by AUT-1 (new fields that add to what is there and take nothing away).
