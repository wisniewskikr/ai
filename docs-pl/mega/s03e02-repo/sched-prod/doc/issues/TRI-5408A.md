## Intent
The client's logo in the booking confirmation email keeps its own aspect ratio. Split from merged ticket IN-5408 (2 reports, two problems).

## Acceptance
- Given a wide logo and a square logo, when the confirmation email is rendered, then each keeps its aspect ratio.
- Given a logo larger than the header, when rendered, then it is scaled down, not stretched or cropped.
- A test renders the email with both logos.

## Boundaries
- Must not change the email text or links.

Allowed by AUT-1 (bug fixes inside the specs, with a test).
