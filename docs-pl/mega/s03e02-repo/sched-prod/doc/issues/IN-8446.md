## Question
Support asks to make booking references 6 characters long because people mistype 10 on the phone (IN-8446). A client's front-desk software matches our 10-character references and they ask us not to shorten them (IN-9898). Do we change the reference length?

## Options
1. Shorten all references to 6 characters, rewriting the stored ones.
2. Keep 10-character references, and make them easier to read out: show them in groups (ABCDE-FGHIJ) and let search accept them with or without the dash.
3. Give new bookings 6-character references and keep the old ones.

## Recommendation
Option 2.

## Why
Because a client told us they rely on 10-character references (IN-9898), which needs a person (AUT-2), and rewriting stored references is rewriting stored data (AUT-2). Option 2 changes neither and helps the phone case.
